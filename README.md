Heatwave Cover Studio — a GTA-style cover maker that runs entirely in the browser, offline. Drop in your own photos and arrange them in up to nine panels across eight layouts: the slanted five-panel poster with gold frames, hero + trio, a four-panel grid, full bleed, triptych, nine-panel mosaic, tilted snapshots, and a diagonal split. Each panel crops by drag and zoom, centred on the face when the browser can find one. Grade the whole cover with a Vice City sunset or neon wash, then finish it like a real poster: a role tag on every panel, a huge retro-chrome title with a neon script subtitle, a tagline, a five-star review quote, an original-artwork seal, a barcode, a billing block, and corner badges. Export a PNG in 1080 × 1080, 1080 × 1350, 1920 × 1080, or 1080 × 1920 — one size, or all four at once — with undo and redo throughout. Four sample photos are included, the display fonts (Anton and Pacifico) ship under the SIL Open Font License, and everything is plain static files: no library, no network request, no Rockstar artwork.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080
```
